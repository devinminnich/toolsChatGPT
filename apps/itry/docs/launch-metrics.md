# iTry launch metrics

The product should optimize for successful gifting, not attention.

Primary measures for a future beta:
- occasions with at least one relevant recommendation viewed;
- recommendation-to-selection rate;
- selected-gift transaction completion rate;
- completed gifts facilitated;
- percentage of recommendations requiring no manual preference maintenance;
- duplicate-risk rate;
- Autopilot veto/change rate;
- delivery-on-time rate for directly fulfilled gifts.

Avoid using recipient gift scores, time-in-app, or raw browsing volume as primary success measures.
