# iTry design notes

The prototype uses a calm purple/charcoal visual system to keep the product feeling personal rather than transactional. The home screen prioritizes the next meaningful occasion, then recommendations, then automation controls. Privacy explanations are placed beside the relevant feature rather than hidden in a policy screen.

Mobile behavior collapses the recommendation grid to one column, preserves navigation, and keeps the preference-helper card reachable. The design intentionally avoids recipient-facing scores, gift grades, or social comparison mechanics.
