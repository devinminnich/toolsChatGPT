# iTry product specification

## Promise
Remember who matters, when they matter, and help the giver choose something thoughtful without requiring the recipient to maintain a wishlist.

## Core behavior
Anyone can join. Connections are accepted between registered users; private contacts cover non-users. Each giver privately defines relationship, budget and Autopilot settings. Common and custom occasions drive reminders/recommendations. Recipient preference evidence stays private; the giver sees only gift candidates and privacy-safe reasons.

Autopilot is opt-in, contact/occasion scoped, has a do-not-exceed amount, and always gives the giver a veto window. Above-limit purchases require explicit approval. Duplicate risk is a warning for manual purchases and prevents automatic purchase until resolved.

Recipients may maintain a private delivery profile that can be used for fulfillment without revealing the underlying address to the giver.

## Business model
Consumer access begins free. iTry earns revenue when it facilitates a completed gift through merchant commissions, affiliate attribution, or later commerce/service fees. Recommendation relevance is not for sale.

## Expansion
Physical products first. Experiences/services and group gifting follow after the core recommendation and commerce loop is proven.
