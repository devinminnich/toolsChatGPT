# Gift Autopilot

Autopilot is explicitly opt-in. It can be enabled per connection/occasion with a hard do-not-exceed amount. An eligible candidate under the ceiling enters a veto window; the giver can buy sooner, choose another item, or skip. No response may allow the in-limit purchase only after the veto period. A candidate above the ceiling never purchases by timeout and requires explicit approval. Possible duplicates are removed from automatic execution until resolved or replaced.
